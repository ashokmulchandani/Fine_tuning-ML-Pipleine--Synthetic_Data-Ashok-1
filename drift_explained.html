🔍 How to Read the Drift Report

✅ No Drift (bars match)
Users still ask what the model trained on.
Model answers are reliable.
Do nothing. Keep monitoring.
❌ Drift Detected (bars mismatched)
Users asking about new topics (COVID, weight loss).
Model was NOT trained on this. Answers will be WRONG.
Action: RETRAIN with new data.

🧠 Drift = the DISTRIBUTION of questions changed. It's not that users ask one new question. It's that the PATTERN of questions shifted — e.g., from "mostly Metformin" to "mostly COVID." The model was never trained on COVID. Its answers will be hallucinated or wrong. Drift catches this BEFORE it becomes a problem.
